01 · Before you begin

Download the latest ACC 2027 Committee Contacts (.vcf) file from the committee's shared folder in Google Drive. A VCF is a standard contact file; you do not need a special app.

Each entry contains the person's name, available phone number, available ACC and personal email addresses, team affiliation, and role or leadership designation.

Privacy: This directory is for authorized committee use. Do not publish the VCF or share its personal phone numbers and email addresses publicly.

02 · Import the contacts

  1. Open the shared link and download the .vcf file to your iPhone (for example, in Files).
  2. Open the downloaded VCF from Files or the app where you received it.
  3. Tap the option to add or import the contacts. Depending on your iOS version, you may see a contact preview and an option such as Add All Contacts.
  4. Choose the contacts account if prompted, then confirm the import.
  5. Open the Contacts app and search for a known committee member to confirm it worked.

If tapping the file only previews it, try Share → Contacts or open it from Files. The wording can differ across iOS versions.

  1. Download the .vcf file to your Android phone.
  2. Open Contacts (often Google Contacts).
  3. Find Organize / Fix & manage / Manage contacts → Import from file. The menu name varies by phone manufacturer and app version.
  4. Select the account where you want the contacts saved, then select the downloaded VCF file.
  5. Wait for the import to finish, then search for a known committee member.

Some Android phones also let you open the downloaded VCF directly and choose the Contacts app to import it.

04 · Read a contact card

Open a contact to see their phone number, email addresses, organization, team, and title. For example, a title might appear as Business Relations — Committee Head (CHO).

05 · Troubleshooting & updates

Nothing appears after import?

Check whether the contacts were imported into a different account, and whether your Contacts app is showing all accounts or contact lists. Try importing the VCF again only after checking, to avoid duplicates.

Search doesn't find the team?

Try the full team name rather than an abbreviation, or search for a person's name. Search behavior is controlled by the Contacts app and may not include every custom field.

Received an updated directory?

Importing a new VCF may create duplicate contacts instead of updating the old entries. Check with the committee coordinator about whether to remove the previous ACC directory before importing a replacement.
